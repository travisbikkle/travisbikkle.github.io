How to Replace the Arm of DJI Mavic Mini

 Travis Bikkle included in Tips
2024-09-28 2024-11-12 About 500 words 3 minutes - views  
Contents
  • Required tools
  • Steps

My son’s drone had broken its front left arm once and had been glued before, so I took advantage of the opportunity to fix it before vacation.

Required tools

  1. Screwdriver
  2. Plastic crowbar or a bank card
  3. Tweezers
  4. Soldering iron, solder and rosin
  5. Glue and electrical tape may be needed, if you accidentally break something
  6. Carefullness and patience

Steps

  1. Unscrew the upper shell screws

  2. Remove the upper shell by using a crowbar or a bank card.

    In order to avoid breaking the upper shell’s, please refer to the following pictures in advance to get a good idea of what to expect:

    Side view:

  3. Unscrew the bottom shell screws

    Again, please refer to the image of the bottom shell and don’t break it

  4. Remove the cover by unscrewing the screws on the bottom black cover plate and pull out the antenna with tweezers

  5. Use a soldering iron to remove the 3 wires connecting the arm to the fuselage

  6. Remove the arm by unscrewing connector that connects the arm to the fuselage and using a screwdriver from the other side to push out the position circled in red.

  7. Remove the antenna, paddles, screws on the back of the motor, and the cover of the arm

    Okay, now you can remove the whole arm.

    Remember, when pulling the three motor wires out of the fuselage, you need to remove the glue stuck to the wires. Be careful not to cut the wires like I did.

    If you accidentally cut the wires, you can solder them back on with a soldering iron and wrap them with electrical tape.

  8. Thread the antenna and motor into the new arm. Thread the antenna back to the bottom and splice it back into its original position. Solder the motor wires back to their original position.

    Since the solder joints are very close together, if you’re not a pro, I suggest practicing a bit more elsewhere, and if you get two solder joints together, don’t hesitate and remove the solder away as soon as possible.

    I’m sure you’ll be able to solder better than I did:

  9. Here comes the hardest step, putting the connector of the arm and fuselage back together. Take care to place the arm at an angle as shown, then press the connector back in firmly.

    Be careful not to let the antenna and motor wires get in the way of the connector

  10. Before installing the upper and lower covers, put the motor, antenna and paddles back on and test for proper function.

    When mounting the antenna, it can be fixed with double-sided tape.

    Your should test:

    Does the motor work, does it take off properly, does the airplane tilt during takeoff and descent.

  11. After the function test is completed, use this to put back the bottom cover, bottom case, and top case.
