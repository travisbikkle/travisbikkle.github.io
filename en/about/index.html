Additional projects I have worked on

Whether it’s a personal project or a company project, I always pay a lot of attention to security, performance and automated test coverage.

It seems I always solve problems by my own, but I’m always happy to take input from colleagues in order to achieve a highly maintainable and well-considered solution.

I like introducing automated tools to the team, or some more effecient and modern ways of development (e.g. Build your own cloud development environment in China using Coder), of course I always consider the acceptance of the team members and urgency of the project.

I also like developing some small tools, such as An Edge browser plugin named Neat, with 5000+ users in China, it helps Chinese users from be bothered by some famous but rogue websites. Sadly I had to stop updating it at a pretty early stage due to force majeure.

Contributions to the community

I used to be more accustomed to only contributing to open source projects that I actually used in my personal projects, but as I’ve gotten to know more about the open source community, I have learned more. Some great projects have a limited number of maintainers, so in the future I would like to contribute more to some great projects based on their issue lists, even if they are not used in my projects.
